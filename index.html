Ask a Question ↗
What kind of cars can I enquire about?

You can enquire about hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs and family vehicles. Our live stock changes, so contact us for current availability, specifications and prices.

Does Happy Wheelz provide PDI inspection?

Yes, PDI inspection is one of the services you can enquire about. Please contact us to confirm service coverage, appointment availability, inspection scope and charges for your vehicle.

Can you help me sell my existing car?

You can contact us about our park-and-sale assistance. We’ll discuss your car, expected price, vehicle details, parking arrangements and applicable terms before proceeding.

What should I check before buying a used car?

Review service records, ownership and registration details, insurance, accident or repair history, tyres, warning lights, engine and transmission behaviour, underbody condition and all documents. Consider an independent inspection and verify records with the relevant authorities.
